I lead with these because they set the foundation for who I am and why I do what I do. As my career evolves, these loves remain constant. 

Now for the nitty gritty: I am a Software Engineer who specializes in quality and also loves creating marketing content. And here is where I tell you the story of how these became my passions. 

I have always loved coming up with app ideas, and wanting to build them myself led me to study computer science at Washington State University (Go Cougs!). Along the way, I fell in love with the problem-solving side of software development and knew I wanted to make a career out of it. 

In true me fashion, everyone around me was becoming a software developer, so I wanted to do something different. I chose test engineering to learn a different side of engineering, and I spent over two years as a Software Test Engineer at Qualtrics. I became a product expert, asking the hard questions to find edge-case bugs and areas for improvement before the product reached customers.

Testing has never stopped me from building. Throughout my experience as a Software Test Engineer, I continued to do work in the software development space, whether it be developing customer-facing improvements to features, building tools, or working on personal side projects. 

You may be wondering (as I often do myself): where in the world does marketing fit into all of this? It turns out the same core thought process drives both testing and marketing. Both require deeply understanding a product and business from the customer's perspective, in ways the people building it don't always get to see. On top of that, marketing taps into my creative side.
